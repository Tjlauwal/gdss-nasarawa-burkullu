About GDSS NASARAWA BURKULLU

GDSS NASARAWA BURKULLU is a Government Day Secondary School committed to providing quality secondary education to students in a supportive, disciplined and conducive learning environment.

The school encourages students to develop strong academic foundations, good character, discipline, confidence and skills that can help them contribute positively to their families and communities.

Our school promotes the values of knowledge, discipline and excellence while encouraging students to pursue their educational goals.
